# Asset sources

## Reachy Mini / Richie

Asset: visual and proportion reference. Source: Pollen Robotics Reachy Mini. Original: https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini and https://store.pollen-robotics.com/collections/reachy-mini. Licence: upstream Apache-2.0; product images are reference-only. Modified: an original lightweight procedural model retains the head, body and antennas, without legs, arms or wheels. Usage: player render model with an independent primitive collider.

## Devoxx robots and venue

Asset: Voxxy, Droid, Biggy sheets and Kinepolis library. Source: https://game.devoxx.be/references.html. Licence: competition reference material; no images are redistributed. Modified: original low-poly interpretations retain the dominant orange Voxxy, tall graphite Droid and stocky blue-gray/orange Biggy cues. Venue geometry compresses registration, exhibition, grand stairs, corridor, auditorium and stage.

No third-party audio, textures or generated meshes are included. Materials, signs and geometry are made at runtime.
